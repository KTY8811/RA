\# Research Question Register



Do not treat candidate questions as final until sufficient evidence has

been collected.



\## RQ001



\*\*Question:\*\* Can externally mounted vibration sensors detect and distinguish

transformer mechanical abnormalities under realistic load variation?



\*\*Status:\*\* Candidate



\*\*Motivation:\*\* To be established from the literature.



\*\*Required evidence:\*\*



\- Physical mechanism connecting faults to vibration

\- Sensor placement evidence

\- Laboratory validation

\- Field validation

\- Influence of load and background noise

\- Comparison with alternative sensing methods



\*\*Main uncertainty:\*\* Whether measured signatures transfer from controlled

laboratory arrangements to operating transformers.



\---



\## RQ002



\*\*Question:\*\* Does multimodal vibration, acoustic and current sensing improve

fault-detection robustness compared with individual sensing modalities?



\*\*Status:\*\* Candidate



\*\*Main uncertainty:\*\* Whether the improvement justifies additional sensors,

synchronisation, data processing and deployment complexity.
